# Photograph credits

Two sets, with different origins and different obligations.

## 1. The site's own photographs of Tetouan

The 21 files in this folder without a `tetouan-` prefix were supplied by Ahmed
for this course. They carry no attribution requirement here and are used as page
banners and faint page washes, indexed in `data/photos.js`.

**This file did not exist before October 2026**, which was an oversight: a
repository that ships photographs should say where they came from. If any of
these came from a source that asks for credit, add it here.

**Colour grade.** Since 9 October 2026 the served copies (`<name>.jpg` and
`<name>-sm.jpg`) carry one shared colour grade, so they sit in TetouTalk's
palette: softer blue skies, greens toward the medina green, calmer golds,
lifted shadows. `design/photos/grade.py` does it; byte-identical copies of the
ungraded files are in `design/photos/ungraded/`. Nothing was added to or
removed from any picture; only colour and tone changed.

## 2. The curated library (Wikimedia Commons)

Twelve photographs chosen for specific teaching purposes, recorded in
`data/photo-library.js`. **Only the first is in this repository.** The other
eleven are links; they are not downloaded, not offline-ready, and must not be
referenced from a page until they are.

### Recorded but NOT installed

Eleven photographs: the Spanish quarter and medina arch, a café, a taxi and a
fountain in Tetouan; a market in northern Morocco (city unconfirmed — never
caption it Tetouan); a bakery and a craftsman in Fes; mint tea and lanterns in
Marrakesh; a meal in Rabat. Each one's author, licence, source and crop guidance
is in `data/photo-library.js`.

**Before installing any of them, read its licence.** Eight of the twelve are
**CC BY-SA 4.0**: attribution *and* share-alike. Share-alike applies to
adaptations, and a cropped, resized derivative in a public repository is an
adaptation. Two are CC BY 2.0 (attribution). Two are CC0 (neither).

That is a decision about this repository's licensing, not a design choice, and
it is Ahmed's to make. Nothing has been installed that carries a share-alike
obligation.

### A note on what these photographs do and do not show

The place labels describe where a photograph was taken, according to its
uploader's own record. They are not linguistic evidence. A bakery in Fes
illustrates buying bread; it attests nothing about how bread is asked for in
Tetouan. Where a location is unconfirmed, the record says so, and the site must
repeat that rather than quietly resolving it.
