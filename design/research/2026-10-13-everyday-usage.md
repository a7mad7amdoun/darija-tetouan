# Everyday Tetouani Darija as Tetouanis use it — usage, social picture, sources, listening leads

Compiled 2026-10-13 for a small private course (one Tetouani teacher, two learners).

**Evidence rule used throughout.** Only text read on a page that was actually opened is reported. Every item gives: source title, the URL opened, and a quotation of at most 25 words copied as written (transcription symbols kept). Nothing is translated or respelled by me unless marked *[my gloss]* or *[inference]*. Search-engine summaries were used only to find pages; they are never cited as evidence. A "COULD NOT OPEN / NOT FOUND" list closes the report.

**Transcription note.** The Zaragoza sources (CORVAM, Vicente, Naciri-Azzouz, Aguadé) use their own broad phonetic transcription: `ʔ`/`Ɂ` = glottal stop, `q` = uvular/velar stop, `ɹ` = approximant r, `ţ`/`ț` = affricated t, `ă ĭ ŭ ǝ` = short vowels, `ḷḷāh` = emphatic *Allah*. They are copied exactly, including inconsistencies between pages.
