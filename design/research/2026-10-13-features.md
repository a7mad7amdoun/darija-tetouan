Scope labels: **mdini** (traditional medina speech) / **tetouan** (city generally) / **north** (shared with Tangier, Jebala etc., as the source says) / **national** / **unknown**. Confidence: high = specialist source states it plainly; medium = inferred, OCR-only reading, or one weaker source; low = Wikipedia / student thesis only.

Note on Singer's 1953 data: his informants were educated Tetouanis who also spoke Spanish; his main informant "was known for still speaking the dialect well, except that he was able to articulate q and sometimes tried to avoid the Tetouan r" (S-T 107). Singer's special signs: ɛ = ʕ, ḫ = x, ʒ = the glottal-like reflex of q, r̄ = the Tetouan spirant r, ṯ/ṭ with underline = affricated t, å ǫ ẹ = his vowel shades.

## 3. Discrepancies between sources (do not resolve silently in the course)

1. **The Tetouan r.** Singer 1953: voiced *spirant* r̄, no emphatic variant, most speakers cannot avoid it (S-T 109). CORVAM: *approximant* [ɹ], mdini only. Vicente 2017 n.28: "uvular". Same sound, three labels.
2. **dkōra.** CORVAM glosses Singer's `dkəɹ/dkōɹa` as 'child/children'; Singer's page says „männliche" and uses `ḫwāti dkōra` 'my brothers'.
3. **māš and ʕād among the young.** CORVAM: māš "seems to be disappearing", ʕād "in clear regression"; Vicente 2017 p.307: pressure "not particularly strong as it continues to exist in the sociolect of younger speakers", with youth examples.
4. **2sg levelling direction.** CORVAM presents the single form as `ţəʕrəf`/`kūl` (masculine shape); Singer 1953 saw the *feminine* `-i` forms spreading to men (S-M 249 n.2).
5. **ʕāyla 'girl'.** CORVAM lists it as a northern Tetouan word; Singer 1953 says Tetouanis themselves preferred bənt because ʕāyla also meant 'prostitute'.
6. **qåddāš.** Singer glosses it 'wieviel?' on p.247 but "'wie groß?' (nicht 'wieviel?')" on p.263.
7. **Wikipedia-only claims** (de- 2sg prefix, ha- future, Tamazight loans) have no support in CORVAM, Singer or Vicente.
