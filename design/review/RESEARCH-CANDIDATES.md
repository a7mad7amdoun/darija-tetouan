# Candidate words and phrases from the 13 October 2026 research

Prepared for Ahmed. **Nothing here is published as a word.** Each line is a form that a researcher recorded from a Tetouani, or that a journalist or glossary wrote down, copied exactly as the source spelled it. The spellings are theirs, not the course's. Before any of these becomes a card it needs Ahmed's own Latin form with stress, Arabic with checked harakat, scope (mdini / tetouan / north), one approved example and a review date. Second-hand items (a thesis citing Sayahi, Moscoso citing Heath) say so.

The two evidence logs are `design/research/2026-10-13-language-contact.md` and `design/research/2026-10-13-everyday-usage.md`. The site shows these forms in the Tetouani guide under "Heard in Tetouan" and "The other languages in it", always labelled as transcription, never as a spelling to learn.

## Already settled by the sources, no card needed

- ntīna for both genders is "the only attested form" in Tetouan (Benítez Fernández & Guerrero 2022). Already taught.
- ḍ as ṭ (băyṭa, mrīṭa) in both city varieties. Already in the guide.
- No Berber loanword in Tetouani was found in any opened source; no Haketia word in present-day Muslim Darija either. Nothing to add.
